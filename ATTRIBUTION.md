# Kural test vocals – attribution

This branch holds a subset of the following datasets, each licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

**Changes made:** a subset of files was selected; audio was converted to 16-bit mono FLAC and cut to at most 45 s; f0 reference annotations were converted to `time_s,f0_hz` CSV (0 = unvoiced) and cut to the same length. Nothing else was altered.

- **dcs** — Rosenzweig, S., Cuesta, H., Weiss, C., Scherbaum, F., Gómez, E., & Müller, M. (2020). Dagstuhl ChoirSet: A multitrack dataset for MIR research on choral singing. TISMIR 3(1), 98-110. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4618287
- **vocadito** — Bittner, R. M., Pasalo, K., Bosch, J. J., Meseguer-Brocal, G., & Rubinstein, D. (2021). vocadito: A dataset of solo vocals with f0, note, and lyric annotations. arXiv:2110.05580. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5578807
- **vocalset** — Wilkins, J., Seetharaman, P., Wahl, A., & Pardo, B. (2018). VocalSet: A singing voice dataset. ISMIR 2018. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1442513

These recordings are used only to test Kural. They are not part of the plugin and are not distributed with it.
